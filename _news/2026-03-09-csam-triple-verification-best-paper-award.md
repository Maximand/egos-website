We are delighted to share that our paper *"Gold Standard or Gold-Plated? Human Practices of Triple Verification in CSAM Takedown"*, by Melissa Rottier, Michel van Eeten, and [Savvas Zannettou](/team/savvas-zannettou), received a 🏆 **Best Paper Honorable Mention Award** at the [2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26)](https://chi2026.acm.org/).

The paper presents a mixed-methods study of how triple verification (requiring three reviewers to agree) is implemented in the classification of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), combining expert interviews, an inter-reliability experiment with Dutch National Police experts, and a focus group.

You can read the paper [here](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3772318.3791039).

*Congratulations to the authors!*
